About AIESEC

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organization that develops leadership among young people through cross-cultural experiences and opportunities.

AIESEC provides young people with opportunities to develop professional skills, experience different cultures and create positive impact in society.

About AIESEC IN NSBM

AIESEC NSBM is the local chapter of AIESEC at NSBM Green University. The club creates opportunities for students to improve leadership, teamwork and communication skills.

Through events, projects and cultural activities, AIESEC NSBM encourages students to become global leaders and connect with people from different backgrounds.
